# COLEMAN 4.9 — Larger CI histories, explicit test stages

Move beyond a single real-data demonstration while keeping every stage and
observation inspectable. COLEMAN now prepares four-build ActiveMQ and Log4j
histories as analytical Parquet and runs their declared native stage views through
the same TCPCI API, policies and resumable checkpoints.

## What you can do

- **Use larger real method histories.** Frozen source recipes preserve 18,901
  ActiveMQ observations and 30,066 Log4j observations with original PR/build,
  suite, class, method and execution identities.
- **Keep environments distinct.** Separate Log4j Ubuntu and Windows native views
  bind exact raw context arrays and build-local node IDs to processed stages.
  All stages remain in analytical files; selecting one view never erases the rest.
- **Audit every native exclusion.** Outside-stage, not-run, unknown-outcome and
  unavailable-duration records retain their original evidence and reconcile with
  the complete source. Reported zero seconds remains distinct from missing data.
- **Run, inspect and resume.** Each four-build view runs Random and UCB with the
  existing native RNFail feedback and checkpoint protocol. Immutable source and
  completion receipts make identical retries reproducible.
- **Recognize missing evidence.** Typed temporal blockers distinguish resolved
  within-report stage correspondence from unavailable executor/outcome/feature
  clocks, expected test universe, verified continuity and concurrency topology.

Use the [complete stage-view guide](https://jacksonpradolima.github.io/coleman/lrts-stage-views/)
for exact source downloads, bounded local acquisition, Parquet schemas, typed API,
native runs, recovery and attribution. Large third-party archives stay outside
Git and the wheel. Recipes independently verify whole-archive, nested-member and
decoded-report bytes; no archived subject repository is executed.

## Compatibility and evidence

The new versioned recipe family has its own producer and artifact namespace.
Existing single-stage LRTS recipes/receipts, raw readers, RTPTorrent workflows,
RunSpec identities and checkpoints retain their semantics. Old artifacts are not
silently migrated. Unknown schemas, producers or Parquet writers reject with an
explicit compatibility requirement.

Stage views are **post-hoc serial simulations**. Reported scheduled labels are
not verified outcome chronology; method-duration sums are not physical parallel
CI savings. Both Log4j views share the same source project/build groups and do not
become independent projects or faults. The profiles retain
`blocked_source_evidence` and `insufficient_evidence`; the diversified corpus,
protected comparisons and confirmatory report in #261 remain ongoing.

Validation covers the installed base wheel outside checkout, actual real-source
Parquet/native/checkpoint workflows, versioned goldens and old artifacts,
round-trips, units/missingness, non-finite rejection, collision guards, forged
provenance, deterministic identities, duplicate conservation, interrupted writes,
concurrent retries and domain/transport import boundaries. This is evidence of a
reproducible pipeline, not an algorithm effectiveness or production-savings claim.
